# Luigi Mari

## Welcome

I'm Luigi Mari, an engineer turned data scientist. After four years in industrial R&D and quantitative product work, I now apply the same discipline to financial markets.

## What this book is

A collection of executable notebooks on systematic strategy research in Python. Every chapter uses real market data, relies on statistics that don't assume normality, and measures classic biases (such as membership lookahead) instead of hiding them. Every number on the page can be re-run and reproduced.

## Background

**Master's Degree** in Risk Management, Models & Data Science for Finance, University of Pisa
Coursework: stochastic processes, statistical learning, econometrics, machine learning for finance, derivatives pricing, credit risk modeling. Thesis: deterministic filtering models and nonlinear machine learning methods for building rotational portfolios that generate robust alpha (full analysis in [this repository](https://github.com/luigi-mari/etf-rotation-thesis-MasterRiskManagement)).

**Bachelor's Degree (BEng)** in Mechanical Engineering, University of Salerno
Specialization in thermodynamics and heat transfer. Coursework: mathematics, physics, fluid mechanics, computer science. Thesis: design of a residential HVAC system based on a quantitative study of thermal loads.

## Industrial experience (2021 – 2025)

Mechanical design and MV cable accessories at an electrical engineering company serving utilities and private customers in more than 50 countries. I worked with the technical department on product engineering and with the sales department on data-driven decision tools. Some of the projects I worked on:

- **Pricing and margin modeling:** Monte Carlo demand-uncertainty model under cost, pricing and production constraints, used to set risk-adjusted margins on international quotes across product configurations.
- **RFQ margin-check dashboard:** redesigned the quote-approval workflow, significantly increasing daily throughput.
- **Thermal regression analysis:** OLS and machine-learning regression on noisy thermal time series to isolate the physical drivers of component behavior, supporting design decisions that reduced unit costs.
- **Failure-mode analysis:** modeling of contact-resistance distributions in HV metallic connectors through mean-reverting SDE simulation to identify critical failure modes.
- **Data automation:** Python pipelines for data extraction, cleaning and reporting, replacing manual analysis across the technical department.
- **Certification support:** statistical analysis of HV lab sensor data in support of IEC product certification.
- **Customer-facing work:** direct engagement with international clients and representation of the company at industry exhibitions.
