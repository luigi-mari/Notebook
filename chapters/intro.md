# Welcome

Hi, I'm **Luigi Mari**. I'm an engineer turned data scientist: four years of industrial work in quantitative modeling, time-series analysis, and data automation, now applied to financial markets. This book is where I build systematic strategies in Python, validate them under honest assumptions, and publish the whole process.

## What this book is

Every chapter here is an executable notebook, not a report: real market data, results checked with statistics that do not assume normality, and the classic biases (like membership lookahead) measured instead of hidden. If a number is on the page, you can re-run it and get it back.

## Background

**University of Pisa** (MRM, Master Risk Management, Models & Data Science for Finance): stochastic processes, statistical learning, econometrics, machine learning for finance, derivatives pricing, credit risk modeling. Master's thesis: a two-layer rank funnel on a 21-ETF universe with systematic IC/ICIR feature validation and walk-forward optimization across sizing schemes; out-of-sample Sharpe of **1.38** with an annualized alpha of **13.4%** (HAC t = 2.87).

**University of Salerno** (BEng, Mechanical Engineering): mathematics, physics, thermodynamics and heat transfer, fluid mechanics, computer science.

Before the Master's I spent four years in industrial R&D on high-voltage electronics: stochastic simulation of failure modes, regression on noisy thermal time-series data that cut component unit costs by about a third, and Python pipelines that replaced manual analysis across the department. In the last stretch of that role I moved into quantitative product work: Monte Carlo models for risk-adjusted margin decisions under cost and pricing constraints, a quote-approval dashboard that lifted daily throughput by 75%, and copper options used to hedge raw-material exposure on international contracts.
