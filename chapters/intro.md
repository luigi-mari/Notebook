# Welcome

Hi, I'm **Luigi Mari**. I'm a mechanical engineer turned R&D data scientist: four years of industrial work in quantitative modeling, time-series analysis, and data automation, now applied to financial markets. This book is where I build systematic strategies in Python, validate them under honest assumptions, and publish the whole process.

I hold a Master's degree in **Risk Management, Models & Data Science for Finance** (University of Pisa), with a focus on quantitative asset allocation frameworks, machine-learning signal validation, and walk-forward portfolio optimization.

## What this book is

Every chapter here is an executable notebook, not a report: real market data, transaction costs included, results checked with statistics that do not assume normality, and the classic biases (like membership lookahead) measured instead of hidden. If a number is on the page, you can re-run it and get it back.

## Chapters

1. **Naked Options Simulation in Python**: Black-Scholes-Merton pricing from a live option chain, GBM Monte Carlo for the naked position path by path, and interactive 3D sensitivity surfaces across strike, tenor, and implied volatility (theta and delta included).
2. **Nasdaq-100 Momentum Waterfall**: a multi-period momentum funnel (12m/6m/3m) rotating through point-in-time index constituents, net of costs, with the full significance battery: HAC t-stats, block bootstrap, walk-forward folds, and the cost of membership lookahead quantified case by case.
